## Independence

This project is independent and is not an official Victron Energy integration.

Victron Energy, VE.Bus, MultiPlus, Quattro, and MK3-USB are names used to describe compatible equipment. They remain the property of their respective owner.

## Why This Fork Exists

The goal is to provide an actively maintained Home Assistant integration with a broader entity set, clearer docs, energy dashboard support, and careful control features for users who connect directly through MK3-USB.
